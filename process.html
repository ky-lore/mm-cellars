Home/Process

Begin, and arrive.

One team carries your cellar from the first sketch to the quarterly service call years later — one phone number, one standard of care.

— Phase i

Consultation & design

It starts with an on-site visit from a cellar designer. We measure the space, understand your collection and how it will grow, and listen to the architectural intent of the home.

You receive a considered design — racking layout, materials, glass and door details, and a refrigeration strategy — before a single board is cut.

  • On-site assessment & heat-load calculation
  • Racking, millwork & glass design
  • Refrigeration strategy & budget
— Phase ii

Construction & craft

Our craftspeople build only wine cellars. Vapor barriers, insulation, custom racking, glass walls, doors, and millwork are executed to a gallery standard — the parts you see, and the parts you never will.

  • Vapor sealing & insulation
  • Custom wood & metal racking
  • Glass walls, doors & finish millwork
— Phase iii

Climate & commissioning

The right system for the room — split, ducted, ceiling-mounted, or self-contained — is specified, installed, and commissioned in-house. We tune it to hold temperature and humidity without a sound.

  • System selection & installation
  • Humidity & temperature commissioning
  • Whisper-quiet operation in adjacent rooms
— Phase iv

Stewardship for life

The relationship doesn't end at handover. Quarterly maintenance, emergency service, and refrigerant work are performed by the same technicians who built the room — for the life of your collection.

  • Scheduled quarterly maintenance
  • Emergency & priority service
  • Refrigerant & component work

Tell us about your space and your collection. We'll schedule an on-site consultation with a cellar designer across Los Angeles and Orange County.
