Refrigeration is where a wine cellar is won or lost. We specify, install, and commission the system your room actually needs — and stand behind it.

— Brand-Agnostic By Principle —

We are authorized for every major manufacturer — and loyal to none.

The right system is the one that fits the room, the collection, and the silence you expect from it — not the one a dealer is trying to move. Because we design, install, and service every brand ourselves, our recommendation is engineering, not sales.

We size to a real heat-load calculation, account for adjacent living spaces, and commission each unit to hold temperature and humidity — the variable most installers ignore.

— System Types —

The right configuration.

Four broad approaches, chosen for the architecture and acoustics of your home.

— i

Split Systems

The compressor lives elsewhere; the cellar stays silent. Our most-specified approach for residential rooms where quiet is non-negotiable.

— ii

Ducted Systems

Air handled remotely and ducted in — ideal when equipment must be hidden and noise kept well away from living space.

— iii

Self-Contained

Through-wall units for compact cellars, cabinets, and retrofits where a full split isn't warranted.

— iv

Ceiling-Mounted

Specialty configurations for architectural constraints — tucked away where floor and wall space is precious.
