— Our Story —

We were never a builder that dabbles in cooling, nor an HVAC company that dabbles in cabinetry.

M&M Cellar Systems was founded on a conviction that a wine cellar is not two projects handed between two trades — it is one discipline. The room and its climate are designed together, built together, and cared for by the same hands for the life of the collection.

Over two decades, that focus has made us the quiet specialists behind some of the finest private cellars in Los Angeles and Orange County — from waterfall glass rooms in Bel Air to hillside refrigeration in Calabasas.

We remain deliberately small, deliberately family-run, and deliberately obsessed with the details most people never see: the vapor barrier, the condensate line, the two-degree swing at 3 a.m. in August.

— What Guides Us —

Everyday mastery.

One Accountable Team

Design, construction, and refrigeration under one roof. One phone number, one standard, no finger-pointing between trades.
